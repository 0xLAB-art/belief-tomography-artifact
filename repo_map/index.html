    2 · Manuscript¶

    The manuscript and its build artifacts are not hosted in this repository — reviewers receive the paper through IEEE. Validation does not require it: the ledger quotes every claim verbatim. The figure data (paper/tops/figs/figdata/, 20 manifest-pinned files) was released from the deposit together with the manuscript on 2026-11-27; the frozen-manifest header documents the scope reduction.
