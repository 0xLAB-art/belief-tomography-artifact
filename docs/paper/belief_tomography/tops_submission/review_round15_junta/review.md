## Summary

This paper (v7, ACM TOPS submission) introduces belief tomography: active black-box estimation of an autonomous agent's behavioral belief profile via attack probes under a deliberate threat model, with a Wilson-interval certification semantics, an impossibility/query-complexity/deception-frontier theory layer, a constructed-foil instrument-validation layer, two live field measurements, and a disclosure-economics layer. Round-15 was the third adversarial pass over a manuscript unchanged since Round 14 (section mtimes Nov 24 01:11-01:37; Round-14 commit 10:06), so every Round-14 reject-grade finding had to be re-verified from primary sources, and the board added a fresh-eyes referee. The verdict is so far the paper's strongest and its manuscript's weakest. The verification forensic panel recomputed every load-bearing number from the frozen artifacts and EVERY ONE reproduces to the digit: dose-response r=0.986-0.990 on 1,500 episodes; test-retest 0.9488; E17 sensitivity 24/25; all field and open-weight Wilson intervals; the 230-flag audit decomposition (112/112 secret-echo flags contain the canary verbatim; 118/118 unauthorized-action flags re-read as refusals; zero destructive-command compliance anywhere); Prop 8.3's closed form reproduces the E16 grid exactly (1,2,8,33,103,549,2057). The theory layer's classic machinery also audits clean (Thms 4.1/4.3, Lemma 4.6, Thm 4.7(iii), Props halflife/reactivity/alloc). But the manuscript's three reject-grade defects all persist, the registration record is worse than Round 14 could show (retroactive date edits inside registration files; a future-dated E20 header; a post-computation E15/E16 registration), and Round 15 adds eleven verified majors the earlier rounds had not found: an estimand that is not the quantity any field estimator computes; an E2/E3 direction inversion that presents refuted positive hypotheses as pre-registered nulls; a caption pool that misdefines a headline number by 2.4x; canned synthetic replies substituted into the deposited transcripts of censored turns; an ethics claim falsified by the paper's own field section; three figures whose captions misstate the runs they decorate; and an EPK live claim its artifact censors. None of this requires retracting a single measured number - and the board emphasizes that the measurement program itself survived everything thrown at it. What fails is the manuscript's account of its own theory and of its own record; that account is what must be rewritten before this work can be accepted at any serious venue.

## Weaknesses

- REJECT-GRADE 1 (verify-critical): Theorem 4.8(i)(a) is false as printed, on the paper's own registered operating points. It asserts detection probability >=1-beta_d when delta>=rho_0 with M>=delta^-2; but rho_0 is a z-multiplied CI half-width, so at delta=rho_0 the level-0.05 two-sample test has power ~0.50, and reaching >=0.95 requires delta>=(z_{0.975}+z_{0.95})sigma_0 = 1.84*rho_0. At the registered E5 cells (delta=0.4,N0=10) and (delta=0.2,N0=40), delta/rho_0 = 1.41, so the best achievable power is 0.788 (0.852 exact UMP) at EVERY horizon M - the printed guarantee is unattainable where the paper claims it holds. Compounding: Table 2's two 'between' rows satisfy delta>=rho_0 and so are predicted 'saturation' by the printed theorem - the table contradicts the theorem on the same page; the appendix proof prints the deceived rate as p-delta while the registered planes shift UP (q=p0+delta, p0=0.30), making the printed model unable to represent half the grid (a negative rate at delta=0.4); and arm (ii) is circular (the 'plausible set' is defined as what the test cannot distinguish, so 'deceptions inside the plausible set are in-channel undetectable' is a definition quoted as a theorem, and the Fig 2 caption cites it as something the data 'requires').
