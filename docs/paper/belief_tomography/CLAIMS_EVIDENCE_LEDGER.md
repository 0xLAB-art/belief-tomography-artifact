## Round-18 corrections (2026-11-26)

A five-seat adversarial review of the IEEE TDSC build re-derived the theory, recounted every table cell from raw artifacts, and audited the manuscript's own record. The following manuscript changes were verified against primary artifacts and are reflected in this ledger; rows above are updated where they were stale.

1. **Onset theorem (M\*).** The printed formula used the conservative `v̄ = max{p(1−p), q(1−q)}` in both variance terms, which makes the "no finite horizon for δ ∈ (0.261, 0.285)" claim false. The exact two-sample variance `q(1−q)/M + p(1−p)/N₀` gives `M*(δ) = (z+z')²q(1−q) / (δ² − Δ²_{β_d})`, finite iff `δ > Δ_{β_d}`; the regime table is unchanged. Recomputed: at (p,q)=(0.3,0.5), N₀=40, δ=0.27 → M\* = 694 (the old text printed ∞).
2. **Exact binomial test power.** 0.807 at (40, 0.2) is not the power of the named test; the level-0.05 one-sided exact binomial with 40 draws rejects at k\*=18 and has power 0.7852 at q=0.5 (0.850 at (10,0.4) verified). Corrected at three sites.
3. **E30 V4-Pro censoring bound.** The identification interval is [0.133, 0.377]: 40 genuine `secret_leak` sessions, 76 censored sessions, overlap 3, so the upper endpoint is (40+76−3)/300. ## Scope, conventions, and honesty rules

- **Scope.** Load-bearing numbers as printed in the manuscript's body sections (`paper/tops/sections/*.tex`): experiment results, audit counts, censoring fractions, intervals, and the theory layer's empirical instantiations. Section references cite the section file and its `\label`. Cosmetic counts (page budgets, bibliography sizes) are out of scope. Where a printed value and the artifact disagree at the digit, **both are recorded**, and the disagreement is named.
- **Paths.** Artifact paths are repository-relative (repository root: `/home/elaref/Documents/research/ragin`). `gauntlet/demo/` (referred to below as `g/d` inside table cells only after full-path first use) is the frozen experiment deposit; `docs/paper/belief_tomography/` holds the day-0 registration files; `data/field/` the live-field sessions; `RAGIN-Retrieval-Augmented-Honeypot-Intelligence/` (referred to as the *nested repo*) holds the live-fleet deployment artifacts.
- **Commits.** Each row's commit is the last commit touching the primary artifact (`git log --format=%h -1 -- <path>`; author date in parentheses). Exceptions, stated explicitly: (a) artifacts **never committed to git** are marked `untracked` — for these the manifest SHA–256 is the only freeze; (b) the nested repo is a separate git repository excluded by the root `.gitignore`, so its artifacts carry **nested-repo** commits; (c) run logs (`*.log`) are git-ignored and untracked. The manuscript itself is untracked by convention (`.gitignore` `/paper/`).
- **Times.** All times and dates are **UTC**; the manuscript's Data Availability states the UTC convention governs, and that registration-header dates are author-local (UTC+2). The ledger records frozen artifact mtimes and commit author dates only as provenance evidence, never as claims about intent.
- **Verification vocabulary.** *Recomputed* = recomputed for this ledger from the frozen artifact (formulas: Wilson 95 % score interval; Clopper–Pearson where the manuscript labels an interval exact). *Transcribed* = read from the frozen artifact's own summary. Rows that could not be pinned to an artifact are in the **Unpinned numbers** section, not silently omitted.
- **Estimator pin.** The frozen estimator/verdict stack is `gauntlet/tomograph.py` + `gauntlet/verdict.py` at commit **8ceb6e0** (2026-11-18), recorded as `estimator_pin` in `gauntlet/demo/E2V1_SUMMARY.json` and in the E6–E10 result files ("frozen `wilson` / `HeuristicJury` at `8ceb6e0`").
